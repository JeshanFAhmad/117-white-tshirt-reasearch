# Research: Plain White T-Shirts and Competitor Analysis

**Brand:** Everyday  
**Research reviewed:** November 15, 2026  
**Product concept:** A plain white crew-neck T-shirt for everyday wear.

## 1. Research Question

How can Everyday present a plain white T-shirt in a way that feels useful and distinct from established basic-clothing brands?

This review compares published competitor product information and proposes possible audiences and website decisions. It does not include customer interviews or usability-test results.

## 2. Competitor Research

### UNIQLO — minimalist everyday essential

**Product:** Unisex Crew Neck T-Shirt

UNIQLO presents this shirt as a modern wardrobe essential. Its product page describes heavyweight cotton jersey, a regular fit, and a clean design intended for everyday wear and layering.

**What the product page communicates:** material, fit, sizing, care, and other product details.

**What Everyday can learn:** Explain the product clearly and show how a simple shirt can work in different everyday outfits.

**Source:** [UNIQLO — Unisex Crew Neck T-Shirt](https://www.uniqlo.com/us/en/products/E422992-000/00)

### Hanes — comfort and durability

**Product:** Beefy-T Unisex Heavyweight Cotton T-Shirt

Hanes emphasizes heavyweight cotton, a roomier cut, reinforced stitching, and a neckline designed to hold its shape. Its product page also provides fabric and sizing information.

**What the product page communicates:** fabric, fit, stitching, and construction details.

**What Everyday can learn:** Practical details can help customers understand what they are buying. Avoid making durability claims unless the actual product construction supports them.

**Source:** [Hanes — Beefy-T Unisex Heavyweight Cotton T-Shirt](https://www.hanes.com/products/hanes-beefy-t-unisex-heavyweight-cotton-t-shirt/o5180)

### Gildan — straightforward basic

**Product:** Heavy Cotton Adult T-Shirt (G5000)

Gildan presents the G5000 as a versatile basic shirt. Its product information highlights cotton fabric, classic fit, multiple sizes, and use for everyday wear or customization.

**What the product page communicates:** fabric weight, fit, available sizes, and product use.

**What Everyday can learn:** A simple product page can still provide useful specifications. Everyday should make its own product details easy to find.

**Source:** [Gildan — Heavy Cotton Adult T-Shirt](https://retail.gildan.com/heavy-cotton-adult-t-shirt/)

### American Apparel — fabric and construction details

**Product:** Fine Jersey Unisex T-Shirt (2001)

American Apparel's specification sheet emphasizes fabric composition, fit, construction, and garment measurements.

**What the specification sheet communicates:** fabric weight and composition, seams, and measurements by size.

**What Everyday can learn:** Specific measurements and construction details are more useful than vague statements such as "high quality."

**Source:** [American Apparel — Fine Jersey T-Shirt 2001 Specification Sheet](https://d1l2kcmc130e06.cloudfront.net/1/specs/american-apparel-2001.pdf)

## 4. Two Possible Brand Positions

These are proposed directions for Everyday, not claims about what customers have said.

### Position A: Affordable everyday basic

- **Main message:** A straightforward shirt for regular everyday use.
- **Website approach:** Clear product image, visible price, simple size selection, and direct product details.
- **Possible strength:** Easy for visitors to understand.
- **Challenge:** Established brands already sell basic shirts, so Everyday needs a clear reason to be chosen.
- **Evidence still needed:** Actual product cost, selling price, fabric, and construction before making value or quality claims.

### Position B: Minimalist wardrobe essential

- **Main message:** One simple piece that can be styled in different ways.
- **Website approach:** Minimal layout, neutral colors, outfit examples, clear fit information, and detailed specifications.
- **Possible strength:** Creates a recognizable brand experience beyond simply selling a blank shirt.
- **Challenge:** The product must support any claims about fit, material, or quality.
- **Evidence still needed:** Final product specifications and genuine photographs before describing the shirt as premium or unusually durable.

### Recommended direction

Start with **Position B: Minimalist wardrobe essential**, while keeping the product information and price easy to understand. The competitor research shows that simple T-shirts are already widely available, so Everyday needs a recognizable presentation and clear information. This is a design recommendation based on competitor pages, not proof of customer preference.

## 5. Two Persona Hypotheses

These are fictional planning tools. They are not real customers and have not been interviewed.

### Persona hypothesis 1: The practical everyday shopper

- **Possible need:** A plain shirt that is easy to wear and understand.
- **Likely questions:** What does it cost? What sizes are available? What is it made from?
- **Website response to test:** Put price, size options, material, and fit information near the product.

### Persona hypothesis 2: The minimalist-style shopper

- **Possible need:** A simple shirt that can be combined with different outfits.
- **Likely questions:** How does it look when styled? What is the fit? Is the design simple?
- **Website response to test:** Use uncluttered product photography and show a few different styling examples.

These hypotheses should be checked with actual feedback before being treated as confirmed audience preferences.

## 7. Design Decisions for Everyday

Based on the current research, the proposed website should:

1. Use a clean, uncluttered layout that supports the minimalist direction.
2. Keep the product name, price, and size selection easy to locate.
3. Include clear product photographs, including front and back views when available.
4. Add material, fit, care, and measurement information once the actual shirt specifications are confirmed.
5. Use neutral colors and consistent typography to support the brand identity.
6. Avoid unsupported claims such as "best quality," "lasts forever," or "most comfortable."
7. Make the shopping flow straightforward, without fake reviews, false scarcity, or invented customer endorsements.

These are proposed design decisions. They have not yet been validated through customer testing.
