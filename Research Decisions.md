## Two Possible Brand Positions

**Position 1: Affordable Everyday Basic**

Everyday could position itself as an affordable clothing brand that offers simple, comfortable white T-shirts for daily use. The focus would be on practical value, easy shopping, and a basic design that works for different occasions.

**Position 2: Minimalist Wardrobe Essential**

Everyday could position itself as a minimalist clothing brand focused on simple, versatile pieces. The white T-shirt would be presented as a wardrobe essential that can be styled in different ways and worn in many situations.

**Recommended Position: Minimalist Wardrobe Essential**

The minimalist position gives Everyday a clearer identity instead of competing only on price. It also supports a clean website design, simple product photography, and straightforward product descriptions. The brand can still emphasize affordability without making low prices its entire identity.

## Persona Hypotheses

These personas are fictional research tools based on possible customer needs. They are not real customers or the result of interviews.

**Persona 1: The Practical Everyday Shopper**

- Wants a basic white T-shirt for regular use.
- Values comfort, affordability, and durability.
- Prefers simple product information and an easy shopping experience.
- Wants to know the price, available sizes, and material before buying.

**Persona 2: The Minimalist Style Shopper**

- Prefers simple clothing without unnecessary designs or graphics.
- Wants versatile pieces that can be combined with different outfits.
- Values clean product photography and a consistent brand appearance.
- Prefers a website that feels organized, modern, and easy to navigate.

**Target Audience**

Everyday will primarily target people who prefer simple, understated clothing and want a versatile white T-shirt without unnecessary designs or complicated shopping experiences.

## Research Finding → Design Decision → Verification

**Research Finding**

Competitor research shows that brands such as UNIQLO offer basic crew-neck T-shirts with straightforward product information. This suggests that customers shopping for basic clothing may value clear descriptions, accessible product details, and simple designs.

**Design Decision**

Everyday will use a minimalist website layout with clear product photography, visible pricing, available sizes, and straightforward descriptions. The design will avoid unnecessary decorations so customers can focus on the product.

**Verification**

A reviewer can check the Everyday website and confirm whether the product price, sizes, description, and images are easy to find. The reviewer can also compare the website's presentation with the research finding and decide whether the design supports the minimalist brand position.
